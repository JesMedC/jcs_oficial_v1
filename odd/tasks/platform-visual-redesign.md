# Platform-wide visual redesign

## Goal
Create a premium, conversion-oriented public portal and a coherent Bitcora-inspired visual system across public, user, and admin surfaces.

## Product decision
The public portal should be visually striking and fluid to attract customers. It should share the platform identity with authenticated surfaces but use calmer storytelling and stronger conversion hierarchy than the dense trading workspace.
